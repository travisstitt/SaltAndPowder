Salt & Powder

The Caribbean, in the age of the buccaneers. You have a sloop at Port Royal, four guns a side and no gold. England, Spain, France and the Netherlands each hold ports and send out merchantmen and warships under their own colours. Take prizes, buy a bigger ship, and choose whose enemy you can afford to be.

Wind
You cannot sail into it. A broad reach, with the wind over your quarter, is fastest. Watch the compass.
Guns
They fire sideways. Turn your beam to the target. With aim assist on, the shaded lanes show where each broadside will fall and turn green when it will hit.
Shot
Round shot breaks hulls. Chain shot shreds sails so prey cannot run.
Prizes
A ship strikes her colours when her hull is battered or her sails are shot away: white flag, sails furled, a white ring around her. Hold fire and sail into the ring to board for the full prize. Sink her and most of it goes down.
Nations
Attack a nation's ships and she turns hostile: her ports close and her navy hunts you. Her enemies warm to you. Governors offer paid commissions in port, and the Spanish treasure fleet, unarmed galleons under escort, is marked on the chart.
Chart
Ports are the yellow marks on the small chart. Open the full chart, with every port named, from the panel at top left; the game pace is set there too.
