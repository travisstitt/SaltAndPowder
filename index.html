Salt & Powder

You have a sloop, four guns a side and no gold. Merchantmen run between five ports with full holds. Take them, spend the plunder in port, and stay ahead of the hunters your name attracts.

Wind
You cannot sail into it. A broad reach, with the wind over your quarter, is fastest. Watch the compass.
Guns
They fire sideways. Turn your beam to the target. With aim assist on, the shaded lanes show where each broadside will fall and turn green when it will hit.
Shot
Round shot breaks hulls. Chain shot shreds sails so prey cannot run.
Prizes
A battered ship strikes her colours: white flag, sails furled, a white ring around her. Hold fire and sail into the ring to board for the full prize. Sink her and most of it goes down.
Pace
Set the game speed to Slow, Steady or Brisk in the panel at top left.
